An EfficientNet-B0 backbone extracts saree motif features into a 256-D L2-normalized embedding for cosine retrieval and verification. Images are resized to 224x224 with geometric and color augmentations to promote palette robustness. A classification baseline and batch-hard triplet metric model are trained on leakage-safe pattern-family groups. Retrieval and verification are evaluated on held-out splits; color robustness is tested synthetically since verified same-design colorway pairs are unavailable.
